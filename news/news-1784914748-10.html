India’s crude refinery throughput rises in June

2026-07-25 01:39

inOil & Companies News24/07/2026

India’s crude oil refinery throughput increased 1% in June compared to the previous month, reaching 5.44 million barrels per day, according to provisional government data.

The June figure translates to 22.26 million metric tons of crude processed. In May, refineries processed 5.21 million barrels per day, or 22.05 million metric tons. The June 2026 throughput matched the level recorded in June 2025, which stood at 5.44 million barrels per day.

Three oil tankers carrying more than 95,000 metric tons of Saudi crude oil destined for India turned back in the Red Sea on Tuesday. Yemen’s Houthi militia has imposed a naval blockade against Saudi Arabia.

Indian Oil Corp (NS:IOC) cancelled the lifting of Iraqi oil from Basrah Oil Terminal. The company cited increased risk and attacks on vessels passing through the Strait of Hormuz.

Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Ltd (NS:MRPL) also cancelled plans to collect oil from Iraq using the Indian-flagged Aframax tanker Desh Gaurav, the company said.

India’s crude oil imports in June dropped more than 12% from May to 19.59 million metric tons, government data showed.Source: Investing.com
