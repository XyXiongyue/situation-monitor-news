Federal Reserve Rate Freeze Sparks Market Drop, Inflation Concerns

Wed, 29 Jul 2026 13:40

The U.S. Federal Reserve (Fed) kept the benchmark interest rate unchanged, while New York stock markets fell across the board. The markets were affected by the Fed’s rate freeze, rising international oil prices, and other factors. In particular, as investors worried that the Fed’s rate freeze could impact the fight against inflation, medium- and long-term government bond yields surged.

On July 29, the Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 2.2%, the S&P 500 Index dropped 1.5%, and the Nasdaq Composite Index declined 1.7% on the New York stock market. The Nasdaq has fallen more than 10% from its all-time high (June 1). The Fed’s rate freeze stirred investors’ concerns related to inflation. Kevin Warsh, Fed Chair, said in a press conference, “We will act without hesitation when necessary and appropriate,” while monitoring high prices. Three Fed officials also argued that the interest rate should be raised by 0.25 percentage points during the rate decision. This situation is by no means a stable price environment, and fears arose that inflation could hit due to the Iran war and global tariffs, among other factors.

U.S. Treasury yields rose, centered around medium- and long-term rates. The 2-year Treasury yield, which moves in the same direction as the benchmark rate, fell by 0.06 percentage points to around 4.21%, but the benchmark 10-year Treasury yield rose by 0.06 percentage points to 4.66%, and the 30-year Treasury yield, a long-term bond, jumped by 0.14 percentage points to around 5.23%. These are the highest levels since 2007. The New York Times noted, “The rise in the 30-year Treasury yield implies a kind of worry about whether the Fed can solve inflation in the long term,” and pointed out, “The sharp increase was triggered by rising inflation expectations.”

In relation to Iran’s attack on U.S. forces, U.S. President Donald Trump ordered a strong response, reigniting tensions in the Middle East, and international oil prices surged. Brent crude jumped 7.9% from the previous close to 90.74 dollars per barrel, and West Texas Intermediate (WTI) crude rose 6.6% to close at 84.46 dollars per barrel. The U.S. Central Command stated that the previous evening, Iran launched a surprise ballistic missile attack on U.S. military bases in the Middle East, but the U.S. military successfully intercepted all of them. In an interview with Fox News, Trump, along with profanity, said, “We will beat Iran to a pulp.”
