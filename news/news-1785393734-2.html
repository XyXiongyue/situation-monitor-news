Germany’s Uniper finalizes 20-year LNG deal with Canada

2026-07-30 14:42

inGeneral Energy News30/07/2026

German state-controlled energy group Uniper finalized a 20-year liquefied natural gas purchase agreement with Canada on Wednesday. The deal builds on a preliminary agreement reached in June.

Under the contract, Uniper will receive 2 million tons of gas annually starting in 2032 from the Ksi Lisims LNG project located on British Columbia’s Pacific coast.

The agreement follows similar moves by other German energy importers. State-owned gas importer SEFE secured Ksi Lisims capacity in May, while Uniper agreed to long-term purchases with Canada’s Tourmaline a year ago.

The deals represent European efforts to establish alternative gas supply routes after Russia’s Gazprom stopped deliveries in 2022. U.S. supplies accounted for 96% of Germany’s LNG imports last year.

The Ksi Lisims project is backed by Western LNG, the Rockies LNG consortium and the Nisga’a First Nation. The project will use hydroelectric dams to power gas liquefaction.

Canada is working to reduce its dependency on U.S. buyers through export agreements with Japan, India and Malaysia. Earlier this month, Canada selected Germany’s TKMS to supply 12 submarines.Source: Investing.com
