Libya halts oil output at key field after protest shutdown

2026-07-29 05:44

inOil & Companies News29/07/2026

Libya stopped production at its El Feel oilfield and partially halted operations at the Wafa field on Tuesday after protesters entered the Mellitah oil and gas complex, the state-owned National Oil Corporation said.

The NOC reported that protesters stormed the facility during the early morning hours on Tuesday, forcing the shutdown of operations at the complex.

Libya’s oil production has faced frequent disruptions due to political and technical issues since the 2011 uprising that removed Muammar Gaddafi from power.

The NOC said it was monitoring with serious concern the developments following the takeover and closure of the complex. The company warned that the shutdown created a major shortage of fuel and gas needed to run power plants, causing several generation units to stop operating.

“The continuation of this situation would increase the risk of instability in the public electricity grid and could lead to more generation units going offline, thereby raising the likelihood of a widespread blackout—or potentially a total grid collapse,” the NOC stated.

Prime Minister Abdulhamid al-Dbeibah directed security forces to retake control of the complex, reopen gas lines, and restart pumping to power plants immediately, according to a statement from the Tripoli-based government.

The El Feel field can produce between 80,000 and 90,000 barrels per day under normal conditions. The Mellitah complex operates as a joint venture between NOC and Italy’s Eni.Source: Investing.com
