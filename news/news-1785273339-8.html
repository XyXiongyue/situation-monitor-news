The shipbroker added that “elsewhere, COSCO Shipping Bulk is understood to have ordered 4x 210,000 dwt Newcastlemaxes at Qingdao Beihai, bringing its orderbook for the design to seven vessels. In the smaller bulk segment, Hong Kong-based Seacon Shipping reportedly contracted 2x 26,700 dwt Handysize bulk carriers at Tsuneishi Zhoushan for USD 33 million each, with deliveries scheduled for 2028 and 2030. Tanker ordering also remained firm. Chinese owner Nanjing Tanker Corporation reportedly booked 4x 50,000 dwt product tankers at Guangzhou Shipyard International, priced at around USD 45.7 million each for delivery during 2028. The vessels will be methanol-ready. In the container segment, Taiwanese liner Yang Ming Marine Transport reportedly returned to Hanwha Ocean for 6x 13,000 teu LNG dualfuel containerships, with deliveries stretching from 2028 to 2030. Although no official contract price has been confirmed, market sources indicate a value in the region of USD 185–200 million per vessel. Elsewhere, Chinese owner Sinotrans is also understood to have ordered 4x 1,800 teu feeder vessels at China Merchants Qingshan, reportedly priced at USD 31.5 million each for delivery in late 2027. In addition, Indian interests Kamal & Adel reportedly placed an order for 1x 1,710 teu feeder at Chizhou Tianyu Shipbuilding for approximately USD 31.5 million. Elsewhere, Greek owner Capital was particularly active in the gas carrier sector, reportedly placing an order for 8x 93,000 cbm LPG/ammonia carriers at Hengli Heavy Industry, with each vessel valued at around USD 125 million and deliveries scheduled during 2028”, Banchero Costa noted.

Meanwhile, in the S&P market, shipbroker Xclusiv noted that “the dry S&P activity was particularly strong this week. The mini cape “HL BALIKPAPAN” – 115K/2012 New Century was sold to Greek buyers, while on the Kamsarmax segment, the sister vessels “NORD ANTARES” – 82K/2022 Yamic and “NORD ANDROMEDA” – 82K/2022 Yamic were sold for USD 37.5 mills each. In the Panamax sector, the “YARRA” – 78K/2015 Sasebo was acquired by Chinese buyers for mid USD 28 mills, and “IVESTOS 8” – 75K/2008 Hudong-Zhonghua changed hands for USD 11.7 mills. On the Ultramax sector, Greek buyers purchased the “SEACON TOKYO” – 66.6K/2023 Tsuneishi Zhoushan for mid USD 41 mills. Further sales included the Ultramaxes “AMIS WISDOM VI” – 61K/2011 Shin Kasado for USD 22.3 mills basis SS/DD passed and “AMIS WISDOM II” – 62K/2010 Oshima for USD 22 mills basis delivery with SS/DD passed. In the Supramax segment, the “HPC ATLANTIC” – 56K/2013 Minaminippon was sold for mid USD 19 mills, the “ST PAUL” – 58K/2010 Tsuneishi Cebu was committed to Vietnamese buyers for mid USD 16 mills, the “CAPT EUGENE” – 55K/2010 Mitsui changed hands for USD 16.6 mills and the “BLUE DIAMOND” – 54K/2008 Ha Long Vietnam was sold for USD 11 mills. The handysize OHBS “LUCKY FINDER” – 37K/2009 Saiki Heavy was sold for USD 13 mills, and the logger “SEAMEC GALLANT” – 32K/2011 Jiangmen Nanyang for USD 9.5 mills basis SS/DD due 10/2026”.

Source: Xclusiv Shipbrokers

The shipbroker added that “this week’s tanker S&P activity was spread across the VLCC, Aframax/LR2 and small tanker sectors this week. On the VLCC segment, the “ALGERIA PROSPERITY” – 318K/2012 SWS was reported sold to clients of Sinokor, with the price remaining undisclosed. In the Aframax/LR2 sector, the “CALYPSO” – 112K/2021 Sumitomo changed hands at an undisclosed price, while the “ELLIE LADY” – 110K/2009 Sungdong was sold to clients of Trafigura for USD 47.5 mills. Also in the same sector, the “VELOS EMERALD” – 115K/2008 Sasebo Heavy found new owners for USD 50 mills. Moving down the sizes, the small tanker “EVA HONGKONG” – 20K/2017 Usuki changed hands for USD 30 mills. Finally, the “HAI XIANG YOU 3” – 5K/2011 Jiangsu Longhai was sold via online auction for USD 1.1 mills”, Xclusiv concluded.Nikos Roussanoglou, Hellenic Shipping News Worldwide
