Red Sea shipping slows after Houthi attacks on Saudi oil facilities

Mon, 27 Jul 2026 03:55

Shipping traffic through the Bab el-Mandeb Strait fell to its lowest level in months after Yemen’s Houthi movement launched attacks on Saudi oil installations, raising fresh concerns over disruptions to global energy supplies and regional maritime trade.

According to shipping data from Kpler, only 11 commodity vessels transited the Bab el-Mandeb Strait on Sunday, marking a sharp decline in traffic through one of the world’s busiest shipping lanes. The slowdown comes as the Houthis seek to block Saudi oil exports, widening the impact of the ongoing US-Iran conflict beyond the Strait of Hormuz.

The disruption has pushed prices for physical crude cargoes in the Middle East, Europe and Africa to their highest levels in two months as traders assess the risks to energy supply chains.

Of the 11 vessels that passed through Bab el-Mandeb, seven were oil tankers. Three entered the Red Sea, including two very large crude carriers (VLCCs) bound for Saudi Arabia’s Yanbu port to load crude oil, while the third was a Russian-linked vessel.

Four tankers exited the Red Sea, including the Hong Kong-flagged VLCCNew Explorer, carrying two million barrels of Saudi and Emirati crude to China’s Ningbo port. Another tanker transported one million barrels of Russian crude to China, while a separate vessel carried approximately 750,000 barrels of Saudi crude destined for Pakistan. Another Hong Kong-flagged VLCC,New Pearl, also departed with two million barrels of Saudi crude bound for China’s Zhoushan port.Sindh News Coverage

Meanwhile, traffic through the Strait of Hormuz remained subdued despite a pause in military strikes between the United States and Iran. Fewer than 10 commodity vessels crossed the strategic waterway each day over the weekend, reflecting continued caution among shipping operators.

Analysts say maritime traffic is likely to recover gradually, as shipowners remain concerned about security risks despite renewed diplomatic efforts to reduce tensions across the region.
