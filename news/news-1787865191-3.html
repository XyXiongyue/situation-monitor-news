The shipbroker added that “International Seaways also reportedly contracted 4x 65,000 dwt product tankers at K Shipbuilding, priced at approximately USD 61 million each, with deliveries scheduled between August 2028 and November 2029. In the container segment, Greek owner Minerva Dry reportedly ordered 2x 3,100 teu containerships at Hengli Heavy Industries, priced at approximately USD 45 million each, with deliveries in June and December 2028. The order includes options for two additional vessels, potentially bringing the series to four units. Centrofin Management is understood to have contracted 2x 6,000 teu containerships at Hengli Heavy Industries, with deliveries in June and December 2028, although no contract price has been reported”, Banchero Costa concluded.

Meanwhile, in the S&P market, shipbroker Xclusiv said in a separate note that “on the Capesize sector, the “MOUNT DAMPIER” – 181K/2011 Imabari was sold for excess USD 38 mills. Moving down the sizes, the Panamax “IVESTOS 9” – 75K/2008 Hudong Zhonghua changed hands for USD 11.75 mills. On the Ultramax sector, Chinese buyers acquired the “AMARYLLIS” – 64K/2013 Yangzhou Dayang for USD 24.3 mills basis SS/DD passed. On the Supramax sector, the “AFRICAN WAGTAIL” – 58K/2013 Dalian Cosco was sold for region/low USD 20 mills, while Middle Eastern buyers acquired the “CBW LIAN YUN GANG” – 57K/2011 Xiamen for USD 10.5 mills at auction basis immediate drydock. Finally, on the Handysize sector, Greek buyers acquired the “ARKLOW SPIRIT” – 36K/2013 Daesun with eco M/E for USD 16.6 mills”.

Source: Xclusiv shipbrokers

In the tanker market, “for yet another week, activity in the tanker S&P market remained subdued, with limited transaction flow across most segments. On the VLCC sector, clients of ADNOC acquired the “OLYMPIC LEOPARD” – 319K/2011 Hyundai Heavy for USD 115 mills. Moving down the sizes, the MR2 “MUMBAI” – 47K/2003 Hyundai Mipo was sold for USD 10.5 mills”, Xclusiv concluded.Nikos Roussanoglou, Hellenic Shipping News Worldwide
