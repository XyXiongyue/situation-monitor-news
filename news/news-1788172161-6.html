2. Bidding Rules:2.1This Bidding will be held without a reserve price. If only one bidder registers for the Bidding, such bidder shall be determined as the Buyer on the Bidding Day, with the purchase price being the Starting Price. If two or more bidders register for the Bidding, the Buyer shall be determined during the course of the Bidding. There is no Right of First Refusal in this Bidding.

2.2The Starting Price is USD 1.7million; Bidding Deposit: USD 0.17 million (or RMB 1.2 million). The Bid Increment shall be in an integer multiple of USD 5,000.

2.3The Purchase Price shall be settled in USD. If the Buyers paid the Bidding Deposit in RMB, such Buyers shall pay another USD 0.17 million separately to the Bidding Company’s designated USD Deposit Account within Three (3) Banking Days after the Auction Confirmation has been signed. The Bidding Company shall return the RMB Bidding Deposit previously paid by the Buyers via the original payment channel to the Buyers within Two (2) Banking Days after receipt of such USD Bidding Deposit (without interest).

2.4The Bidding Deposit of unsuccessful bidders will be refunded within Two (2) Banking Days after the end of the Bidding, and each such Bidding Deposit will not accrue any interest. Bank or other charges incurred in relation to the remittance of each Bidding Deposit shall be borne by the relevant unsuccessful bidders.

2.5The Bidding will commence from 1600 hours on the Bidding Day (Beijing Time) and will continue until 1625 hours on the Bidding Day (Beijing Time) after which time (A) if there are further bids after 1625 hours then the Bidding will only be deemed completed when 5 minutes elapses from the time of the last entered bid or (B) if there is no new bid offered 5 minutes thereafter, the Bidding will be deemed completed.

2.6“Banking Day”means a business day (excluding Saturdays and Sundays) on which banks are open for general business at the places where the Sellers, the Bidding Company and the Escrow Agent are registered, as well as the locations of their respective account-opening banks. The time of this bidding activity shall be subject to Beijing Time (unless otherwise expressly stated).
