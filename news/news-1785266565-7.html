US Fed Meeting Live Updates: Why inflation remains the Fed's biggest concern

The Federal Reserve aims to keep inflation at around 2% over the longer run. When prices rise too quickly, households lose purchasing power and borrowing costs tend to increase.

Although inflation has eased from its peak, policymakers remain cautious because persistent price pressures could force interest rates to stay elevated for longer. Officials have repeatedly said they need greater confidence that inflation is moving sustainably back to target before easing policy.

US Fed Meeting Live Updates: What is the Fed's 'dot plot'?

The “dot plot” is a chart published by the Federal Reserve four times a year that shows where each policymaker expects interest rates to be over the next few years.

Each dot represents one Fed official’s interest-rate projection. Investors study the chart for clues on whether policymakers expect rate cuts, rate hikes or rates to stay higher for longer. The July meeting does not include a new dot plot; the next one is due with the September projections.
